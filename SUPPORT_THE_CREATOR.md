# Support the Creator

Hi, I'm Asif Raza, a chemistry student and researcher building **100 Days of Computational Chemistry**: one small, working, documented tool per day for students and researchers. Everything here is free and MIT-licensed, and it will stay that way.

If these tools save you time, there are a few ways to help, and most of them are free.

## Free ways to help (these matter just as much)
- **Star the repo** so other students can find it.
- **Share it** with a labmate, a classmate or your department group.
- **Open an issue or a pull request** if something breaks or you have an idea. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Cite it** in your thesis or paper using [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff). GitHub's "Cite this repository" button gives you the format.
- **Tell me what you built with it.** I'd love to hear it.

# Code of Conduct

This project is a place for chemistry students and researchers to share small, useful tools and learn from each other. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background, experience level, institution or country.

## Our standards

Things that help make this a good place:
- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to beginners.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Crediting people and sources. Cite data, equations and code you build on.
- Keeping discussion focused on the project and the science.

Things that are not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, spam or repeated off-topic posts.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Passing off other people's work as your own.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, Asif Raza ([@asifverse4](https://github.com/asifverse4)), through the contact details on his GitHub profile. Please include links or screenshots if you can. Reports are handled privately and with care, and the person reporting will not be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful. The aim is first to fix the problem and keep the space welcoming, not to punish.
